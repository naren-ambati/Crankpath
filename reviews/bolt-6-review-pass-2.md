## 3. Specific questions of the brief

- **Can the self-management note still read as reassurance?** No. It states an observation of repo-set facts and a conditional, and `none named` says what crankpath named. It can be planted (m1), and it is not capped (M1).
- **Planted upstream name and labels on an unrelated workload, rendered by an Application on the cycle:** the note appears, naming that object, with the conditional. The conditional is honest as a conditional; the disclaimer misses the name, and the object need not be on the cycle (m1). Planting many such objects floods the explanation (M1).
- **Mermaid on NOTHING ANALYSED:** a `text` fence of the M4 lines, no `Cycles:` line, no `drawn:` line, no diagram, exit 2. Checked by the CLI on an empty folder and by 400 seeded Reports forced to that state (1,200 documents, none with a Mermaid fence). No clean-sounding claim.
- **Mermaid on gap runs:** `drawn: every node and edge of the graph as read (...); the run has gaps, listed above, so the estate may hold more` or the capped form `the graph as read ... is over what Mermaid draws by default`; "the whole graph read" appears only for ANALYSED (1,200 documents of the gap state across default, `maxEdges 3`/900 characters and zero caps: 0 contain it; the 400 forced-ANALYSED default documents do). No remaining clean-sounding claim, apart from the odd empty diagram (n1).
- **Limits derived, not picked?** Both come from published defaults and the 21 is the same screen derivation as before. The values are not invented, but the edge-list use breaks the stated basis (m4). Capped lists say how many are left out for nodes (`first 21 of N`), edges (`the other K are in the JSON output`), paths (`and N more nodes back to node 1`), dependencies and dependents; they do not for the four lists in M1.
- **Does the stop cut the assumptions and not-modelled close?** No: in every run (400 disjoint cycles, 4,000 disjoint cycles, 60 rings of 40, a 20,000-node ring, a 3,000-spoke hub, plant2) the output was at most 1,024 lines and ended with `Assumed ...` and `Not modelled ...`. It does cut the cycle's own closing line when a planted block is too big (M1).
- **Real parser on the tip's outputs:** `mermaid@12.3.0` under jsdom parsed 2,401 of 2,401 Mermaid documents (400 seeds x gap, forced ANALYSED, over-cap and zero-cap forms, plus the Vault document); the 1,200 NOTHING ANALYSED documents carry no Mermaid fence; the negative controls (two broken documents) were rejected. Rendering to SVG, and so how the entity codes display, was not run.
